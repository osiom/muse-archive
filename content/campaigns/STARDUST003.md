A Berlin garden, a record for Ukraine. For one Saturday, a garden in Rixdorf slows down under headphones — a whole day of music, art and encounter grown around a single album, all of it in support of the rebuilding of Ukraine.

## The work

La Luna 007, by Silat Beksi.

La Luna 007 is a mini-LP by Silat Beksi, a musician born and raised in Ukraine and shaped by downtempo and ambient. He wrote it in the middle of the war, and it carries the war the way music can — not as a report, but as something felt: grief, distance, the stubborn fact of home. Released on La Luna, the Berlin sublabel Marco Malpeso runs under Wex, it began as one person's way of speaking. That is where everything else started.

Memo, co-founder of cocoex, and Marco initiated the campaign together, and it grew past them almost at once. A wider circle of artists, most of them Ukrainian, took up the same subject in their own languages — painting, installation, performance, reading — until a single album had opened into a whole day. Memo's painting was made alongside the record, the same feeling in another form, and it hangs beside the prints it gave rise to. Nothing on the day stands apart from the cause: every work shown, every performance, every sale — down to the Peng raffle — carries part of the day toward Repair Together and the ground they are putting back together in Ukraine.

## The event

Silent Rixdorf, Berlin · 22 August 2026 · from 15:00

The day is built around the release of La Luna 007. From mid-afternoon into the evening the garden fills slowly: the label's back catalogue moves across the headphones, installations open, performers take the space, food and drink go round, and Repair Together show the room what their work in Ukraine actually looks like. By evening the new record plays in full, end to end, on every pair of headphones at once. Whoever is there is the first to meet the work — and the first to carry it further. Entry is 5 €, headphones included.

### On the day

- Memo Vithana × La Luna — the original painting behind the La Luna 007 cover, with prints
- Pulkit Gandhi — *Fragments of the Forgotten* — a participatory installation that grows with every hand that touches it
- *Fabeln aus der Ukraine* (Fables from Ukraine) — a decentralised search for traces
- La Luna Discography — Mixtapes 001–006, across the headphones
- Diana Dochiia — *Walls* — body-movement theatre
- Yuliia Syzonenko, Kateryna Kravchenko & Sofiia Onishchenko — *Destruction of Illusion* — joint installation
- Saniia Bludova — *Steine* (Stones) — reading performance
- Silat Beksi — live, and a full listening session for La Luna 007
- Peng raffle, drawn before the evening presentation
- Food, drink and activities throughout

### Afterparty — Some of Us

When the garden closes, the night moves on. Some of Us, a Wex showcase, opens from 23:00 at a secret location in Neukölln, revealed on the day. Presale is open — link via Linktree.

## The Comet

The record and the artworks are what turn a day of attention into something that lasts. They are sold to support Repair Together, a volunteer-led group that has worked across Ukraine since 2022 — more than 100 clean-up actions, over 30 homes rebuilt, and upward of 2,500 volunteers who keep showing up.

## Impact

A share of every sale goes to Repair Together, turning a record and a garden full of art into hands and materials on the ground in Ukraine. The figures will be recorded here once the campaign closes.
