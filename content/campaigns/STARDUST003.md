## The work

*Can a room full of art rebuild a home?*

La Luna 007 — *History of great people* — is a mini-LP by Silat Beksi, a musician born and raised in Ukraine, shaped by downtempo and ambient. Released on La Luna, a Berlin sublabel of Wex, it confronts the war head-on. La Luna and Memo, co-founder of cocoex, initiate the campaign; a wider circle of artists builds it out — installations, performances, readings — each one turning the album's subject into a room you can stand in. The record and Memo's painting were made in parallel and shown as one gesture: both built to be sold, both pointing their proceeds back home.

## The event

Silent Rixdorf, Berlin · 22 August 2026 · from 15:00

A day built around the release of La Luna 007 — *History of great people*. Across the afternoon and evening: a listening session for the full album, art installations, theatre and reading performances, food, and a presentation of Repair Together's work in Ukraine. The audience is the first to meet the work, and the first to carry it forward. Entry is 5 €, headphone rental included.

## The Comet

The record and the artworks are sold to raise funds for Repair Together, a volunteer-led group helping Ukrainian communities hit by the war. Since 2022 they've run over 100 clean-up events, rebuilt more than 30 homes, and brought together more than 2,500 volunteers.

## Impact

A percentage of every sale goes to Repair Together, turning a record and a room full of art into hands on the ground in Ukraine. Figures will be recorded here once the campaign closes.
